# Data protection impact assessment template

## Processing description

The intended processing links contactless screening, wearable trend summaries, health-worker actions, specimen results and ADaM notifications by a pseudonymous patient identifier. The registry identity mapping is isolated from the operational platform.

## Purpose and lawful basis

Purpose per processing activity: to be confirmed with the Ministry and data controller.

Lawful basis, consent wording and consent version: to be confirmed before participant onboarding.

## Data subjects and categories

Contacts under outbreak follow-up, screening encounters, non-diagnostic vital-sign trend summaries, specimen references and limited identity/contact details in the isolated registry.

## Recipients and transfers

ADaM exchange remains disabled beyond approved standalone export until the interface and lawful basis are agreed. Cross-border transfer is disabled. Deployment must keep health data processing and storage in Kenya.

## Necessity and minimisation

Only the minimum allow-listed notification fields are eligible for exchange. Raw wearable streams are not part of platform or ADaM contracts. Small aggregate cells are suppressed in analytics.

## Risks and mitigations

Assess mistaken identity, unauthorised reveal, missed or delayed alerts, duplicate notifications, loss of offline data, wrong configuration, clock drift, re-identification in small cohorts, and failure to honour subject requests. Record likelihood, impact, owner, mitigation and residual risk in the risk register.

## Rights and requests

Document access, rectification, restriction and erasure request routes, responsible role, statutory deadline and evidence of completion.

## Consultation and approval

Consult the controller, Ministry surveillance unit, ICAP and ODPC as required. Approval status is to be confirmed with each authority.
