# Known anomalies

- Live Go and Python service adapters are not yet connected to persistent application repositories, a broker or an identity provider.
- The ADaM wire format and endpoints require confirmation from the Ministry of Health and ICAP.
- FHIR mappings require review against current Digital Health Agency implementation guides before they are finalised.
- Operational console resources and endpoints are contract targets; production authorization and data persistence remain to be implemented and verified.
- Data retention periods, lawful basis, consent text, data controller roles and ODPC registration status require authority and legal review.
