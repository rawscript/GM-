# Incident response runbook

1. Record the incident identifier, discovery time and accountable incident lead.
2. Preserve relevant audit and security telemetry without copying personal data into general monitoring systems.
3. Contain affected credentials, certificates, workloads or export routes using the approved emergency procedure.
4. Assess the affected data, people, recipients, systems and service window with the data controller.
5. Maintain the statutory notification workflow, including the 72-hour ODPC notification window where applicable.
6. Record decisions, approvals, notices, remediation and evidence in the incident register.
7. Verify restoration, rotate affected keys and credentials, and complete a post-incident review before re-enabling exchange.
